# P2Poolv2 Weekly Meeting Notes

Where we keep our discussions, notes, and decisions.

## Add New Meeting

To add a new meeting note, copy the `template.md` or `template.org` file and rename it to the format `YYYY-MM-DD.{md,org}`. Replace the placeholders with the appropriate dates and details.

## Why

To maintain a consistent, searchable record of our weekly meetings that ensures all team members can find historical discussions and decisions easily.
